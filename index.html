How the Square Footage Calculator Works

Square footage is simply area, and for any rectangular space the formula is length × width. Measure both sides in the same unit — usually feet — multiply them together, and the result is the area in square feet. For an L-shaped or irregular room, split it into rectangles, calculate each piece separately, then add the sub-totals together for the full floor area.

If your measurements are in metres or inches, convert first: one square metre equals 10.764 square feet, and there are 144 square inches in a single square foot. When buying materials, it is standard practice to add a 5–10% waste allowance on top of the raw square footage to cover cuts, offcuts and mistakes.

Example: a rectangular living room measuring 15 ft by 12 ft has an area of 15 × 12 = 180 square feet — enough to price flooring or paint for that room.
Example: an L-shaped kitchen is split into a 10 ft × 8 ft section (80 sq ft) and a 6 ft × 4 ft section (24 sq ft), giving a combined total of 104 square feet.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate square footage?

Multiply the length by the width of a rectangular space. For example, a 12ft × 10ft room is 120 sq ft.

What is square footage used for?

Square footage is used for flooring, painting, real estate listings, heating/cooling calculations and building permits.

How many square feet in a square metre?

1 square metre = 10.764 square feet. 1 square foot = 0.0929 square metres.

How do I measure an irregularly shaped room?

Break the space into simple rectangles, calculate the area of each one separately, then add the results together. This works for L-shaped and multi-section rooms.
